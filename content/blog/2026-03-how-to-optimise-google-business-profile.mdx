If you're running a local business in the UK, your Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is one of the most powerful free marketing tools at your disposal. When someone searches for services like yours in your area, a well-optimised profile can mean the difference between appearing prominently in local search results or being invisible to potential customers.

In this comprehensive guide, we'll walk you through exactly how to optimise Google Business Profile to maximise your local visibility, attract more customers, and grow your business.

## What Is Google Business Profile and Why Does It Matter?

Google Business Profile is a free tool that allows businesses to manage how they appear across Google Search and Google Maps. When optimised correctly, your profile appears in the "Local Pack" – those three businesses Google highlights at the top of local search results, complete with maps, ratings, and key information.

For local businesses, this visibility is invaluable. Studies consistently show that the majority of consumers use Google to find local businesses, and many never scroll past the first page of results. If your profile isn't optimised, you're essentially handing customers to your competitors.

## Setting Up Your Google Business Profile Correctly

Before you can optimise, you need to ensure your profile is set up properly from the start. Here's what you need to do:

### Create or Claim Your Listing

If you haven't already, visit [google.com/business](https://www.google.com/business) and either create a new listing or claim an existing one. Many businesses discover that Google has already created a basic listing from publicly available information – you'll need to claim ownership to take control.

### Complete the Verification Process

Google requires verification to ensure you're the legitimate owner. This typically involves receiving a postcard with a verification code at your business address, though some businesses may qualify for phone or email verification. Don't skip this step – unverified listings have limited functionality and won't rank well.

### Choose the Right Business Categories

Your primary category is crucial for ranking in relevant searches. Choose the category that most accurately describes your core business. You can add secondary categories, but your primary selection carries the most weight. For example, if you're primarily a [web design agency](/services/web-design), "Website Designer" should be your primary category, even if you also offer SEO and software development services.

## Essential Information to Complete

Now comes the critical part: filling in every relevant field with accurate, detailed information.

### Business Name and Address

Use your official business name exactly as it appears on your signage and legal documents. Don't stuff keywords into your business name (like "Joe's Plumbing – Best Plumber in Manchester") – Google can penalise this practice.

Your address must be consistent with what appears on your website and other online directories. Even small discrepancies (like "St" vs "Street") can confuse Google and harm your rankings.

### Phone Number and Website

Use a local phone number whenever possible rather than a mobile or national number. This reinforces your local presence. Your website URL should link to your homepage or, if you have multiple locations, to a location-specific page.

### Opening Hours

Accurate opening hours are essential, particularly since Google often displays these prominently in search results. Keep these updated during holidays and special events. Consider adding "special hours" for bank holidays or seasonal changes.

### Business Description

Your business description (up to 750 characters) is your opportunity to explain what makes your business unique. Include relevant services, your area of coverage, and key differentiators. This is one area where you can naturally include important keywords, but focus on writing for humans first.

## How to Optimise Google Business Profile for Better Rankings

With the basics in place, here are the advanced optimisation strategies that will help you outrank competitors.

### Optimise Your Photos and Videos

Visual content significantly impacts customer engagement. Businesses with photos receive 42% more requests for directions and 35% more click-throughs to their websites compared to those without.

Upload high-quality images including:

- Your logo (appears as your profile picture in searches)
- Cover photo (appears prominently in your profile)
- Interior and exterior photos of your premises
- Photos of your team
- Photos of your products or completed work
- Video tours or introductions (these stand out in search results)

Aim to upload new photos regularly – Google favours profiles with fresh, recent content.

### Collect and Respond to Customer Reviews

Reviews are perhaps the single most important ranking factor for local search. They also heavily influence whether potential customers choose your business over competitors.

Actively encourage satisfied customers to leave reviews. You might:

- Send follow-up emails after completing work
- Include review requests on receipts or invoices
- Train staff to ask happy customers directly
- Make it easy by providing direct links to your review page

Just as important as collecting reviews is responding to them – both positive and negative. Thank customers for positive reviews and address concerns raised in negative ones professionally. This demonstrates that you value customer feedback and can actually turn negative reviews into opportunities to showcase excellent customer service.

Google also notices review velocity and recency. A steady stream of recent reviews signals that you're an active, thriving business.

### Use Google Posts Regularly

Google Posts allow you to share updates, offers, events, and news directly on your profile. These appear in your listing and can include images, calls-to-action, and links.

Regular posting (aim for at least weekly) shows Google that your business is active and engaged. Posts can also help you rank for additional keywords and give potential customers reasons to choose you over competitors.

### Add Products and Services

Don't overlook the Products and Services sections. These allow you to showcase what you offer with descriptions and pricing. For service-based businesses, this is your opportunity to detail each service and include relevant keywords naturally.

If you offer [SEO services](https://www.saunders-simmons.co.uk/services/seo/), for instance, create a detailed service listing explaining what's included, who it's for, and what results clients can expect.

### Leverage the Q&A Feature

The Questions and Answers section allows potential customers to ask questions directly on your profile. But here's a tip many businesses miss: you can add your own questions and answers.

Proactively populate this section with common questions your customers ask. This not only helps potential customers find information quickly but also gives you another opportunity to include relevant keywords and showcase your expertise.

### Enable Messaging

If you can respond promptly, enable the messaging feature. This allows potential customers to contact you directly through your Google Business Profile. Quick responses can capture customers who might otherwise move on to a competitor.

## Maintaining and Monitoring Your Profile

Learning how to optimise Google Business Profile isn't a one-time task – ongoing maintenance is essential.

### Regular Updates

Keep your information current. Update opening hours for holidays, add new photos monthly, create regular posts, and ensure your services and products reflect current offerings.

### Monitor Insights

Google provides valuable insights about how customers find and interact with your listing. Review these regularly to understand what's working and identify opportunities for improvement. Pay attention to:

- How customers search for your business (direct searches vs discovery searches)
- Where customers are viewing your profile from
- What actions they take (website visits, direction requests, phone calls)
- Photo view statistics

### Stay Alert for Suspicious Activity

Unfortunately, competitors sometimes attempt to sabotage listings by suggesting incorrect edits. Enable notifications so you're alerted to any suggested changes and can quickly reject inaccurate information.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

As you work to optimise your Google Business Profile, watch out for these pitfalls:

- **Inconsistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone)** across your website and other directories
- **Choosing categories based on what you want to rank for** rather than what you actually are
- **Creating multiple listings** for one location (this can result in penalties)
- **Keyword stuffing** in your business name or description
- **Neglecting reviews** by not responding or not encouraging them
- **Setting and forgetting** rather than maintaining your profile regularly
