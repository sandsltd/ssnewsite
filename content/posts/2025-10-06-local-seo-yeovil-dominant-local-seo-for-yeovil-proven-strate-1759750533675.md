### Local Search Signals: GBP, NAP, and Local Pages

Your Google Business Profile (GBP, formerly Google My Business) is the storefront of your online presence in local search. A complete, actively managed GBP profile boosts visibility in maps and local packs, earns trust through reviews, and provides a direct line to potential customers. Alongside GBP, precise location pages (for Yeovil and nearby Somerset towns) signal to search engines that you serve specific local areas. These pages should clearly show your address, hours, and the services you offer.

Consistency matters. Your business name, address, and phone number (NAP) must align everywhere—GBP, your site, and local directories. In Yeovil, where a handful of local players compete in the same space, even small inconsistencies can cost you visibility and clicks. That’s why our approach includes rigorous NAP validation and structured data on your site so search engines understand where you operate.

### Local Citations and Local Authority

Citations—mentions of your business on other local websites—help confirm your existence in the local ecosystem. We focus on high‑quality, relevant local sources across Somerset and the South West.
