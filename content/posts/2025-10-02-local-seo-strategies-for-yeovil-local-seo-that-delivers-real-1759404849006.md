### NAP Consistency and GBP Optimisation
Nap refers to Name, Address, Phone number — the core business identifiers that search engines rely on to verify your local presence. In Yeovil, even small inconsistencies (like a street name written as Street or St) can chip away at trust and ranking signals. The best practice is to present a single, canonical version of your NAP across all online profiles and your own site. That means matching the address you display on your website with what appears on Google Business Profile and major local directories.

Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is a critical asset in Yeovil’s local search ecosystem. A well-optimised GBP includes current hours, accurate location, a thorough description of your services, high-quality photos, and posts that highlight seasonal promotions relevant to Yeovil’s market. It’s not a one-and-done task; it requires regular updates, fresh photos, and reviews management. This isn’t DIY territory; it’s the kind of careful calibration Saunders Simmons has refined through years of work with Yeovil clients.
