### Secure Hosting and Environment Hardening

Your hosting environment is the playground where every threat unfolds. We partner with trusted UK-based providers and tailor the hosting stack for security from the ground up. ### Transport Layer Security (TLS) and Encryption

We make sure your site uses up-to-date TLS certificates and strongest available ciphers. Encryption isn’t optional on today’s websites; it’s table stakes for protecting customer data in transit. In practice for Yeovil firms, this means shoppers entering payment or personal details stay private, and Google and other search engines treat you as a trusted site – which helps your rankings a little too. If you’ve not refreshed to a modern TLS setup recently, you’ve probably got a weak point that attackers can exploit.

### Web Application Firewall (WAF) and Perimeter Defences

A properly configured WAF blocks many automated attacks before they ever reach your code. We tailor the rules to your WordPress, Magento, Shopify, or bespoke build, and we keep it tuned for the specific traffic patterns you see in Yeovil and Somerset. This matters because small businesses often experience bursts of legitimate traffic (think local events, market days, or a popular advertising campaign) that mustn’t be mistaken for an attack. A good security partner filters the noise and stops threats.

### Patch Management and Vulnerability Scanning

Software updates are boring but essential. We implement a disciplined patch management process so your CMS, plugins, and server components are kept current. Regular vulnerability scanning identifies misconfigurations and weaknesses before they become breaches. And if we do find something, we’ll fix it quickly, with you in the loop so you know exactly what’s happening and why.

### Backups, Recovery, and Business Continuity

Backups aren’t glamorous, but they’re the backbone of resilience. We set up automated, encrypted backups with reliable offsite storage and tested recovery processes. In practice, that means if something does go wrong, you’re back online with minimal downtime, not days of disruption. This is especially important for Yeovil retailers and service businesses that rely on online bookings or e-commerce payments.

### Access Control and Identity Management

We help you implement sensible access controls: least-privilege user permissions, multi-factor authentication, and secure password practices. In a small team, this can feel tedious, but it’s a critical line of defence. The right controls stop accidental data leakage and reduce the risk of insider threats, which are more common than you might think.

### Incident Response Planning

You’ll never be 100% risk-free, but you can be 100% prepared. We create tailored incident response plans for your business and run tabletop exercises so your team knows what to do the moment something looks off. That means faster containment, less damage, and calmer customers.
