### Patch Management and Software Updates
Plugins and CMSs evolve. Patches close known holes that attackers exploit. A professional service keeps systems up to date, tests updates for compatibility, and ensures patches are applied promptly—without causing downtime for your Yeovil site.

### Access Control and Authentication
Strong, unique passwords, MFA, and role-based access control prevent compromised logins from turning into a breach. We implement strict access policies and ongoing monitoring to detect anomalous activity fast.

### Backups and Disaster Recovery
Backups are your safety net. We plan and test regular backups, confirm that restore procedures work, and define recovery time objectives (RTOs) and recovery point objectives (RPOs) so you know what to expect if something does go wrong. And yes, we verify backups in production to be sure you’re never caught out.

### Web Application Firewall (WAF) and DDoS Protection
A WAF blocks common attacks at the edge, reducing the chances of data breaches or downtime. We also implement traffic monitoring and rate limiting to protect against DDoS attempts during busy periods like the Christmas market season in Yeovil.

### Incident Response and Regular Drills
Even with the best controls, incidents can happen. A professional partner provides an incident response plan, roles and runbooks, and regular drills so your team knows how to respond quickly and calmly—minimising disruption and protecting customer trust.

### GDPR Compliance and Data Handling
Security and privacy go hand in hand. We help you map data flows, minimise data collection where possible, and document security measures for audits. It’s not about ticking boxes; it’s about building consumer confidence in Yeovil and Somerset.

A: Start with a strong TLS configuration, regular patching, and MFA for admin accounts. These basics dramatically reduce risk and create a solid foundation for everything else.

Q: How often should a security audit be done?
A: A professional audit should be done at least annually, with interim checks after major updates or new feature launches. Seasonal peaks like the Christmas period in Yeovil justify more frequent checks.

## Direct Answer: Quick Security Checklist for Yeovil Websites

- Use HTTPS with a valid SSL certificate and HSTS; enable TLS 1.2+ with strong ciphers.
- Perform regular patching of all software, plugins, and themes.
- Enforce MFA for all admin accounts and strong password policies.
- Implement a Web Application Firewall and DDoS protections.
- Regularly back up data and test restores; confirm recovery objectives match your business needs.
- Conduct annual security audits and continuous monitoring with real-time alerts.
- Ensure GDPR-aligned data handling and clear privacy notices.
- Train staff on phishing awareness and safe data practices.
