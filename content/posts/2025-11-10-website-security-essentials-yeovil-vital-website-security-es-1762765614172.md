## The Core Security Essentials Every Website Needs
There’s no one-size-fits-all in security, but there are six core pillars that every Yeovil business should consider as non-negotiables. These are the foundations Saunders Simmons builds upon for clients in Yeovil, Somerset, and the surrounding areas.

### 1) Encrypt Everything: HTTPS and TLS
Look, a basic SSL/TLS certificate isn’t enough in isolation, but it’s non-negotiable. HTTPS encrypts data in transit, protecting customer information, login credentials, and payment details. Today, modern sites use TLS 1.2 or TLS 1.3 with HSTS to prevent protocol downgrades. We ensure certificates are properly configured, renewed automatically, and bundled with best-practice configurations. ### 2) Keep Software and Plugins Up to Date
Your CMS, eCommerce platform, and plugins are high-value targets for attackers when they’re out of date. Routine updates patch known vulnerabilities, close exploit paths, and reduce the window of opportunity for adversaries. Our approach includes a live patching cadence, a tested update schedule, and quick-turn upgrades when new vulnerabilities are disclosed. This isn’t something you do once; it’s an ongoing discipline that protects your site while you focus on growing your Yeovil business.

We’ll tie this into your broader strategy with our [web development services](https://www.saunders-simmons.co.uk/services), ensuring compatibility with critical business processes and avoiding feature regressions during updates.

### 3) Strong Authentication and Access Control
Passwords alone aren’t enough. We advocate multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all admin access, role-based access control, and strict session management. In practice, this means fewer compromised accounts and better control over who can publish content, manage products, or process payments. Local teams in Yeovil often juggle multiple responsibilities; MFA makes it easier to secure critical accounts without slowing your workflow.

We incorporate MFA and least-privilege principles into your site’s operational posture as part of our security-first design ethos—something you’ll see reflected in our [web design Yeovil projects](https://www.saunders-simmons.co.uk).

### 4) Reliable Backups and Disaster Recovery
Backups aren’t glamorous, but they’re essential. We implement frequent, encrypted backups and test restore processes so you can recover quickly after an incident. That means less downtime, faster recovery, and a smoother customer experience during peak seasons in Somerset. In our experience with Yeovil-based clients, automated backups combined with tested restoration procedures dramatically cut out-of-pocket recovery costs and keep your revenue stream intact.

Interestingly, data shows that many SMBs underestimate the value of backups until they’re forced to react during a disaster. We help you move from a reactive stance to a prepared one, with clear RPOs and RTOs suited to your business needs.

### 5) Perimeter Protection: WAF, Firewalls, and DDoS Resilience
A Web Application Firewall (WAF) plus a strong hosting environment acts like a moat around your site. It blocks common attack patterns (SQL injection, cross-site scripting, and other web app flaws) and helps you ride out traffic spikes without service degradation. For Yeovil merchants who rely on online sales during busy periods, DDoS protection and robust firewall rules are not optional; they’re part of stabilising your revenue during high-volume weekends and promotions.

We offer a security-conscious hosting approach and a WAF-enabled stack as a standard component of our web design services Yeovil, keeping your site resilient against bot traffic and nasty scrapes from competitors or opportunists.

If you take payments on your site, you’re dealing with sensitive data. Meeting PCI DSS requirements and handling personal data in line with GDPR is non-negotiable for UK businesses.
