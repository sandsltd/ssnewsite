## How We Measure ROI in Web Projects

ROI isn’t a mystery. It’s a set of tangible metrics that connect design decisions to outcomes.

### The metrics that matter
Here are the core measures we track for Yeovil businesses:

- Traffic quality: Are visitors matched to your offer, not just random clicks? We monitor source, intent, and on-site behaviour.
- Engagement: Page views per session, average session duration, scroll depth, and interaction with key elements.
- Conversion rate: The share of visitors who take a planned action (booking, contact form, quote request, or sale).
- Lead value and close rate: How many leads convert into paying customers, and how those customers contribute to revenue over time.
- Customer lifetime value (LTV): Do new customers return, buy again, or upgrade services?
- Speed and accessibility metrics: Core Web Vitals, time to first byte, and accessibility conformance.

These metrics aren’t abstract. We connect them to your bottom line by mapping every design decision to a measurable outcome. For Yeovil firms, that means clearer enquiries, higher-value clients, and longer customer lifecycles.
