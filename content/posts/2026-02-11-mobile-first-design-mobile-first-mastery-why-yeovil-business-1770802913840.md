## Why Mobile-First Design Is No Longer Optional
Mobile-first design is about prioritising the small screen—from typography to navigation to load times. In Yeovil and across Somerset, shoppers, clients, and partners are interacting with brands on smartphones first. If your site looks great on a 27-inch monitor but feels clunky on a 6-inch screen, you’re losing potential customers before they even start scrolling.

A few straightforward truths sit behind the numbers:

- People are impatient with slow pages. Even a seconds’ delay costs you conversions. In 2018, Google highlighted that mobile users often abandon pages that take too long to load, and the trend has only strengthened since. In practical terms for Yeovil businesses, you’re competing with local retailers and national brands who’ve got speed dialed in.
