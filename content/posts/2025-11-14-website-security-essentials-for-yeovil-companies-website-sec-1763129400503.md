- Strong encryption and TLS everywhere: No shortcuts. Encryption protects data in transit and helps meet GDPR expectations. This is non-negotiable for e‑commerce and lead capture forms alike.
- Secure hosting and infrastructure: We assess hosting environments and ensure configurations minimize exposure to common attack vectors.
- Web Application Firewall and monitoring: A WAF blocks known threats while ongoing monitoring flags unusual activity before it becomes a problem.
- Regular vulnerability management: We coordinate patching of vulnerable components and keep dependencies up to date so attackers can’t exploit known flaws.
- Secure development and review: For Yeovil projects, we embed security thinking into design and code reviews so security isn’t bolted on later.
