### Claim and Optimise Your Google Business Profile (GBP)

Your Google Business Profile is often the first touchpoint for local searchers. In Yeovil, where people are choosing between a handful of nearby options, a fully optimised GBP can be the difference between a customer calling you or moving on to a competitor. Key elements include accurate business name, address, and phone (NAP) consistency, hours, category selection, photos that reflect your shop or office, and regular post updates about seasonal openings or promotions.

We’ll tailor your GBP to highlight what matters most to Yeovil customers—whether that’s same-day delivery, click-and-collect, or free consultations—and ensure your listing aligns with UK regulations on advertising and consumer protection. And yes, reviews matter. A steady stream of genuine reviews boosts visibility and trust, especially when new customers in the Surrounding areas are weighing options.

You can use GBP updates to feature seasonal campaigns—think summer promotions for local services or winter opening hours around the Somerset festive period. It’s a simple, powerful signal to Google that your business is active and relevant in Yeovil.

### Build Local Citations and Maintain NAP Consistency

Local citations are mentions of your business across the web (directories, industry sites, local press, and social profiles). The goal is consistent NAP across all platforms. In Yeovil, you’ll want to wire up listings in relevant UK directories and local business resources, while also ensuring your name, address, and phone number match what appears on your site and GBP. Inaccuracies kill local rankings and confuse potential customers.

Our approach focuses on the right mix of high-authority local directories and proven industry sites, all aligned with your GBP and website. We also monitor these listings and correct any drift—like a shift from a PO Box to your actual street address—which helps your local presence stay credible and discoverable.
