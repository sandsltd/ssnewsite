## A Simple Comparison: Desktop-First vs Mobile-First

- Desktop-first focuses on large screens first; mobile is an afterthought. This often means constrained mobile experiences, slower mobile pages, and higher bounce rates for mobile visitors.
- Mobile-first starts with the smallest screen and scales up for larger devices. Benefits include faster mobile load times, clearer navigation, and better conversion on phones.
- In practice, mobile-first design gives Yeovil businesses a stronger foundation for local search, social sharing, and mobile conversions, while still delivering a polished desktop experience.
- The outcome? Higher engagement, more enquiries, and a healthier bottom line.

**What is mobile-first design?**
Mobile-first design is about creating websites starting with the smallest screens, then expanding to larger devices. It prioritises fast load times, clear navigation, legible typography, and accessible interfaces on phones first, then scales up for desktops and tablets.

Because most local searches happen on mobile, and customers expect a smooth, quick experience on their phones. A mobile-first site improves local search rankings, boosts conversions, and makes it easier to run timely marketing campaigns in Yeovil and surrounding areas.
