## A quick compare: mobile-first vs desktop-first outcomes

| Area | Desktop-first approach | Mobile-first approach (recommended for Yeovil) |
|---|---|---|
| Page speed emphasis | Desktop speeds; mobile often slower | Core speed optimization prioritised for mobile, faster load times on slow networks |
| UX focus | Large screens, expansive layouts | Thumb-friendly navigation, legible text, simplified flows |
| SEO impact | Good but dependent on mobile performance | Strong mobile-first SEO benefits via fast, accessible experiences |
| Conversion potential | Strong when desktop users convert | Higher mobile conversions; captures mobile shoppers and local searchers |
| Maintenance | Separate desktop/mobile variations possible | Unified design; responsive by design and easier to maintain |
