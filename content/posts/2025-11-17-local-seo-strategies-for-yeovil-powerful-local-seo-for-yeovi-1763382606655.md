### 1) Optimize Your Google Business Profile (GBP) for Yeovil

Your GBP is often the first interaction a potential customer has with your business online. It’s not just a listing; it’s a mini storefront that can appear in Google Maps, local search results, and the Knowledge Panel. A well-optimised GBP includes:

- Accurate business name, address, and phone number (NAP) that match what’s on your website and local directories.
- A detailed description that includes Yeovil-specific terms and services you offer in Somerset.
- High-quality photos and, where relevant, a short video that showcases your premises, team, and capabilities.
- Regular posts highlighting promotions, seasonal services, events, or community involvement in Yeovil and the surrounding areas.
- Encouraging and responding to reviews to demonstrate active engagement and trustworthiness.

In Yeovil, GBP optimisation is particularly important because it directly influences how your business appears in Maps and local packs for queries like “plumber Yeovil” or “restaurant near Yeovil town centre.” A professional approach ensures your listing is complete, compliant with UK business regulations, and optimised for conversions. ### 4) Local Schema Markup and Structured Data

Schema markup is like a recipe card for search engines. It helps Google understand the context of your content, your business location, and what services you provide—particularly important for local queries.

For Yeovil businesses, the practical pieces include LocalBusiness schema, Organization schema, and Service schema for the offerings you provide in Somerset. Implementing schema correctly can improve your chance of appearing in rich results, local packs, and knowledge panels. A professional team will ensure the right types, properties, and values are in place, while keeping you compliant with evolving UK guidelines.
