## The Core Security Foundations Your Website Needs

There are no silver bullets, but there are solid foundations. The good news is, you don’t need to become a tech whiz to benefit from them. You just need a partner who can translate security into clear business value. Here’s the practical, non-technical toolkit we deploy for Yeovil clients.

### 1) Secure Hosting and Lifecycle Management

- What it means for your business: A solid hosting environment with up-to-date software, patch management, and regular backups. This reduces downtime and protects your data. It also makes your site faster, which matters for user experience and search rankings.
- Saunders Simmons approach: We select hosting with hardened security, monitor uptime, perform routine patching, and test backups so you’re never left scrambling after an failure.
- Local relevance: In Yeovil and Somerset, reliable hosting keeps your seasonal campaigns online through busy periods and helps you meet customer expectations during peak shopping times.

### 2) Transport Layer Security (TLS) and Data Encryption

- What it means for your business: SSL/TLS ensures data between your visitors and your site is encrypted, building trust and meeting browser expectations. It also signals to search engines that you’re a legitimate site.
- Saunders Simmons approach: We implement and manage SSL certificates, enable HTTP/2 or HTTP/3 where possible, and ensure certificates are renewed automatically to avoid warnings.
- Local relevance: The padlock icon and trusted certificates reassure Yeovil customers handling payments or contact forms, especially when local shoppers are weighing domestic versus online options.

### 3) Data Backups and Recovery Planning

- What it means for your business: Regular, tested backups mean you can recover quickly from ransomware, user errors, or plugin failures without losing essential data.
- Saunders Simmons approach: We design a robust backup strategy with versioned snapshots and rapid restoration procedures, plus tested disaster recovery plans.
- Local relevance: In Somerset, where many SMEs rely on online channels during seasonal campaigns, fast recovery helps you minimise downtime and protect revenue.

### 4) Web Application Firewall (WAF) and DDoS Protection

- What it means for your business: A WAF filters malicious traffic and blocks common attacks, helping keep your site available even during traffic spikes or bot-driven assaults.
- Saunders Simmons approach: We configure WAF rules aligned to your tech stack, monitor for unusual patterns, and tune protections as your business evolves.
- Local relevance: A resilient Yeovil site stands up to attempted disruptions from competitors or opportunistic attackers trying to exploit busy shopping periods.

### 5) Access Control, MFA, and Secure Credentials

- What it means for your business: Strong authentication and least-privilege access reduce the risk of compromised accounts.
- Saunders Simmons approach: We implement multi-factor authentication (MFA) for admin access, enforce strong password policies, and audit access regularly.
- Local relevance: This is especially important if you’ve got multiple staff members handling your site during busy seasons or promotions.

### 6) Regular Vulnerability Scanning and Penetration Testing

- What it means for your business: Regular checks catch known weaknesses before attackers do.
- Saunders Simmons approach: We run scheduled vulnerability scans and, when needed, provide targeted penetration testing to validate security controls.
- Local relevance: With UK regulations tightening data protection, proactive testing helps you stay compliant and avoid costly fixes after a breach is detected.

### 7) Compliance and Privacy by Design (GDPR, PCI, and Beyond)

- What it means for your business: Clear data handling, consent workflows, and PCI-aligned payment processes reduce regulatory risk and build consumer trust.
- Saunders Simmons approach: We embed privacy-by-design principles into your site and work with you to document data flows, cookies, and consent choices.
- Local relevance: Yeovil businesses serving customers across Somerset benefit from a compliant, trust-first approach that differentiates you from competitors.
