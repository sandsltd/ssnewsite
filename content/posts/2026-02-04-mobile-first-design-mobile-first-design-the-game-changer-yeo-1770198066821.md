What makes mobile-first design so powerful? It flips the traditional approach on its head. This forces essential information to be front and centre, speeds up load times, and creates intuitive navigation that matches how people actually use their devices. The result? Higher engagement, better user experiences, and more conversions.

This is precisely why Google’s move to mobile-first indexing is so consequential. Since 2018, Google prioritises mobile versions of sites in ranking and indexing.
