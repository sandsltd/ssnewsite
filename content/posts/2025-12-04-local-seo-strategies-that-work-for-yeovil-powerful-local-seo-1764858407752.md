### 3) Local Citations and NAP Consistency

Consistency of Name, Address, and Phone (NAP) across all local directories matters. We audit and harmonise citations across key UK platforms and niche directories relevant to your industry. When NAP data is inconsistent, search engines doubt your legitimacy. In Yeovil and Somerset, we prioritise citations on platforms that matter to your customers and the UK market, ensuring your business appears credible in search results and is easy to contact.

### 4) On-Page Local Signals

We implement clear local signals on your website: location-based pages, embedded maps, service areas (where relevant), and user-friendly contact paths. The aim is to guide Google and your customers to the right next step—booking, calling, or visiting your Yeovil location. This isn’t about cramming keywords; it’s about structuring on-site content so it reads naturally to humans and signals clear locality to search engines.
