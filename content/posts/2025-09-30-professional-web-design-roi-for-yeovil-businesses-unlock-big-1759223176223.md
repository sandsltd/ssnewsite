## How to Measure ROI From Web Design (Without the Guesswork)

ROI isn’t a mystical KPI; it’s a straightforward calculation that you can track with the right metrics and a good partner. Here’s how we help Yeovil businesses quantify the value of professional web design.

### Define clear business goals up front
- Decide what success looks like: more enquiries, more online orders, more booked appointments, or higher-value clients. Align this with your marketing plans and seasonal campaigns in Somerset.
- Agree on the time horizon. Commonly, 6-12 months is a solid window to see meaningful shifts in conversions and revenue.

### Track the key performance indicators (KPIs)
- Conversion rate on key pages (product pages, service pages, contact pages).
- Lead quality and inquiry volume from forms and calls.
- Revenue per visitor and average order value.
- Site speed and mobile usability metrics (Core Web Vitals).
- Return on ad spend when combined with PPC or social advertising.

### Use a simple ROI formula
ROI = (Net Profit Attributable To The Website / Cost Of The Website) x 100
In plain English: if a refreshed site costs £12,000 upfront and delivers £40,000 additional profit over a year, that’s a strong 233% ROI. The exact figure will vary by sector, season, and how well you market the site, but the framework holds.
