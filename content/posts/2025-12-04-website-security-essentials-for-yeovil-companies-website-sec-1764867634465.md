If you’re investing in a professional security programme, you’re buying a framework that protects your site in layers—not a quick patch. Here’s what that looks like when you partner with an experienced team, rather than chasing quick fixes.

### A layered security architecture that actual humans can manage

Think of security as layers: secure hosting, a robust SSL/TLS setup, strong access controls, application hardening, a web application firewall (WAF), and reliable backups. The goal is to reduce surface area for attackers while making ongoing management straightforward for your team. With Saunders Simmons, you get a service that covers:

- Secure hosting with hardened configurations and regular uptime monitoring
- SSL/TLS certificates and enforcement of HTTPS everywhere
- A web application firewall to block common threats before they reach your site
- Regular patching of core CMSs, plugins, and dependencies
- Role-based access control and password hygiene across your organisation
- Daily or near-daily backups with tested disaster recovery drills
- Incident response planning and a clear playbook for quick restoration

The result? Fewer incidents, faster recovery, and greater confidence for your customers.
