## The Essential Security Toolkit for Professional Web Design in Yeovil

There’s no one-size-fits-all. But there is a reliable toolkit that works for most Yeovil businesses when it’s implemented by a partner who knows the local landscape and regulatory environment. Here are the core layers we build into every project, from small business sites to complex e‑commerce platforms.

### 1) Secure Hosting and TLS Everywhere

Right at the foundation, we choose hosting that’s tuned for security and performance. That means always-on TLS (SSL) certificates, HTTP/2 or HTTP/3 where possible, and strict transport security. This isn’t optional—it's the first impression your customers get when they land on your site and the baseline for search engine trust. And yes, we integrate this into our standard Yeovil web design services to ensure every site launches with solid security baked in.

### 2) Patch Management and Software Updates

Plugins, themes, CMS core—if you’re using WordPress or a bespoke CMS, regular updates are a must. We implement a disciplined patch management process so your site isn’t exposed by known vulnerabilities. This is an ongoing service, not a one-off task, and it’s especially important for small Yeovil businesses that rely on a lean IT footprint.

### 3) Web Application Firewall (WAF) and DDoS Protection

A good WAF acts like a sieve, letting good traffic through and blocking bad requests before they reach your pages. For Yeovil companies with ecommerce or lead-generation forms, this can be the difference between peak-season uptime and a site grinding to a halt. We configure a WAF as part of our ongoing security posture, with protections tailored to the traffic patterns you actually experience in Somerset and the surrounding areas.

### 4) Backups, Disaster Recovery, and RPO/RTO

Backups aren’t optional; they’re insurance. We set up regular automated backups, offsite storage, and tested restoration processes so you can recover quickly from breaches, ransomware, or just accidental deletions. We define recovery point objectives (RPO) and recovery time objectives (RTO) that align with your business needs in Yeovil—so you’re never left guessing how long recovery will take.

### 5) Strong Authentication and Access Controls

Admins should use multi-factor authentication, and access should be the minimum necessary for each role. This reduces the risk of compromised admin accounts and protects customer data. We embed role-based access control into the site’s architecture and provide clear admin guidelines so your team stays aligned.

### 6) Secure Development Practices and Code Reviews

Security isn’t something you bolt on after a site goes live. We bake secure development practices into every project—from design reviews to code reviews, vulnerability testing, and threat modelling. For Yeovil firms that depend on bespoke apps or custom extensions, this approach saves time, reduces risk, and keeps you on the right side of UK data protection requirements.

### 7) Regular Scanning and Penetration Testing (Pentesting)

Ongoing vulnerability scanning and periodic external pentests identify issues before attackers do. We partner with trusted security providers to deliver objective assessments, with findings prioritised by impact and likelihood. This isn’t a one-off audit; it’s a scheduled part of our service for Yeovil clients.

### 8) Incident Response Planning and Training

Despite best efforts, breaches can occur. A clear incident response plan helps you respond quickly, meet notification obligations, and protect customer trust. We help you define roles, notification timelines, and containment steps—and we can run tabletop exercises so your team knows what to do when the siren goes off.
