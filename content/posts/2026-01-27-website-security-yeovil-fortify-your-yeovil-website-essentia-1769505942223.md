## The Core Security Essentials Your Yeovil Website Needs

### 1) Strong, Certificate-Backed HTTPS and Modern TLS
A secure site begins with an up-to-date SSL/TLS certificate and modern transport encryption. It’s not just about a padlock; it’s about preventing data eavesdropping, tampering, and impersonation. A mature security stance also includes HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS) and regular certificate renewals to avoid unexpected warnings for your customers.

### 4) Access Controls and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
Least-privilege access and MFA dramatically shrink the attack surface. In practice, it means staff can only access what they truly need, and even when credentials are compromised, attackers struggle to move laterally. It’s a no-nonsense defence that’s become a baseline expectation for serious businesses in Yeovil and Somerset.

We’ll build role-based access into your site and admin panels, and we’ll guide you through sensible MFA options that fit your team’s workflow. This is the kind of protective discipline you’d expect from a professional web design Yeovil partner who thinks about risk, uptime, and customer trust.

### 5) Web Application Firewall (WAF) and Security Monitoring
A properly configured WAF is like a security guard at the digital door. It blocks common attack patterns, blocks suspicious IPs, and helps you spot automated scanning before it becomes a breach. Paired with 24/7 monitoring and alerting, you get proactive protection rather than firefighting after the fact.

You don’t need to be a security expert to have this in place. Our team integrates WAFs with your hosting and content management system so you can focus on growing your Yeovil business instead of worrying about a dark corner of the internet.

We scale security from the ground up, across every page, form, and integration. What are the essential steps to secure a small business website? Here’s a concise answer you can reuse in a briefing with your team or your IT partner:
