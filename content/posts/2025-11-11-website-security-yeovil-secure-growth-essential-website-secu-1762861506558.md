## Core Security Essentials for a Yeovil Website

Look, I’m not going to bog you down with IT jargon. Here’s the practical, outcomes-focused core you need, tailored for Yeovil and Somerset.

### Secure Hosting And Regular Backups
- A robust hosting environment is the foundation. We’ll choose a secure, well-maintained hosting platform with automatic, encrypted backups. In practice, you’ll want daily backups with a quick recovery plan so you can restore in minutes, not hours.
- Outcome: faster recovery after incidents, minimal downtime, and peace of mind during seasonal spikes like the pre-Christmas rush or school holidays when traffic climbs.

### Encryption, TLS, And HTTPS By Default
- A secure site starts with TLS everywhere. It protects data in transit and signals trust to visitors. We’ll migrate and maintain HTTPS across the entire site, including forms and checkout paths.
- Outcome: higher customer confidence, better SEO signals, and compliance with data protection expectations for UK customers.

### Patch Management And Software Updates
- WordPress, plugins, themes, and server components should be patched promptly. We’ll implement a managed update cadence to close known vulnerabilities before attackers exploit them.
- Outcome: reduced risk of drive-by attacks and malware injections that can tank your rankings and bring your site offline.

### Access Controls And Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Admin accounts get strict access controls and MFA. We’ll enforce role-based access, unique credentials, and event logging so you know who touched what, when.
- Outcome: substantially lower risk of credential stuffing and insider mistakes that lead to data exposure.

### Web Application Firewall And Malware Monitoring
- A WAF blocks common attack patterns before they reach your code, while ongoing malware scanning detects unusual changes quickly. We partner with trusted security vendors and tailor rules to your site’s tech stack.
- Outcome: fewer hack attempts getting through, faster detection of breaches, and shorter remediation times.

### Secure Development Practices And Code Review
- Even if you’re not building a site from scratch, new content and features should be reviewed for security impact. We embed security into design reviews and QA checks so flaws don’t slip in.
- Outcome: long-term resilience as your site evolves with your business needs.

### Incident Response, Recovery, And Business Continuity
- We’ll define an incident response playbook, including who to notify and how to restore services. Practically, you’ll have predefined steps and timelines to keep customers informed and minimise revenue loss.
- Outcome: lower downtime during breaches, smoother customer communications, and faster return to normal operations.

### Data Protection And GDPR Readiness
- You’ll have minimised data collection, proper data retention policies, and clear consent mechanisms. We align with GDPR requirements and UKPECR expectations so you stay compliant as you grow.
- Outcome: fewer regulatory headaches and more confident customers who trust your brand with their data.
