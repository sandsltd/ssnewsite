What is considered essential website security for small business?
- A layered approach that includes secure hosting, TLS encryption, patch management, access controls, backups, monitoring, and a tested incident response plan. It’s not just about one tool; it’s about a cohesive strategy.

How often should I audit security on a Yeovil site?
- A quarterly security audit is a sensible baseline for most businesses, with a more frequent review during peak seasons or after major site changes. We tailor this to your risk profile.

Does SSL/TLS matter for e-commerce in the UK?
- Yes. TLS encryption protects customer data during transit and is a foundational trust signal for shoppers and search engines alike. It’s non-negotiable for e-commerce.

- Don’t delay. Notify your security partner, document what happened, and follow your incident response plan. Quick containment minimises damage and regulatory risk.
