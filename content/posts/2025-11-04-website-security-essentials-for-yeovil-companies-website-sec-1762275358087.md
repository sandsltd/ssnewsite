### Access, Identity, and Control
- Robust authentication and access control. Limiting who can change your site is essential. We implement role-based access controls, strong password hygiene, and optional two-factor authentication for your admin accounts. This isn’t a DIY job; it’s a governance matter and a best practice that protects your business.
- Least privilege and changes tracking. Every action is logged, so you know who did what and when. In Yeovil or Somerset, this accountability helps with audits and incident response.

### Ongoing Patching and Configuration Management
- Software updates and patching are part of standard operating practice. A professional security program means your CMS, plugins, and server software get updates on a lifecycle that minimizes disruption and maximizes protection.
- Configuration hardening. Default settings are convenient but risky. We tailor configurations to reduce attack surfaces, ensuring your site runs efficiently while staying secure.

### Monitoring, Detection, and Response
- 24/7 monitoring for anomalies. Security isn’t a once-a-year exercise; it’s ongoing. We watch for abnormal traffic, suspicious login attempts, and unusual file activity—while you focus on customers in Yeovil and Somerset.
