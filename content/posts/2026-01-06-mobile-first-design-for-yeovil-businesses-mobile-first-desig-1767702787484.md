## A Simple Comparison: Mobile-First vs Desktop-First (With a Twist)

| Aspect | Mobile-First Design | Desktop-First (with responsive) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary focus | Speed, usability, and clarity on small screens | Content density and desktop experience first |
| Load time | Optimised for mobile networks (4G/5G) | Can be heavier; performance tuning still needed |
| Conversion paths | Short, obvious, thumb-friendly CTAs | May require more steps on mobile |
| Accessibility | Built-in accessibility from the start | Accessibility needs deliberate checks |
| SEO impact | Strong, due to mobile-first indexing | Good if fully responsive, but less optimal if clunky on mobile |
