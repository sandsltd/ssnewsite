Five Security Essentials You Can’t Do Without (When You’re Running a Yeovil or Somerset Website)

Here’s a practical, non-technical checklist you can discuss with a professional partner in Yeovil. It isn’t a how-to guide; it’s a quick yardstick to ensure you’re covered.

- SSL/TLS and HTTPS Everywhere: Your site should default to HTTPS, with valid certificates that don’t trigger browser warnings. This is the baseline for trust and data protection. We’ll set this up from day one as part of your web design project in Yeovil.
- Regular vulnerability assessments and penetration testing: You need ongoing testing by qualified testers who understand web app security. It’s not a one-off exercise; it’s a continuous cycle that catches new risks as they emerge.
- Backups, disaster recovery, and robust restore procedures: Daily backups, off-site storage, and tested restore plans mean you’re back online with minimal data loss after any incident.
- Access control and staff training: Role-based access ensures people only see what they need. Regular training reduces the risk of phishing and social engineering.
- Secure coding and dependencies management: We vet plugins, themes, and modules for security flaws, patch vulnerabilities promptly, and keep dependencies current.
