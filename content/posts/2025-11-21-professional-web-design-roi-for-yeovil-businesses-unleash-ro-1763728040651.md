### What Constitutes ROI in Web Design?

Put simply, ROI from web design is the economic value you gain from investing in a new site minus the cost of the project. In practical terms, this means improved conversions, higher average order value, better lead quality, and lower customer acquisition costs over time. A robust ROI framework looks at:

- Direct revenue: Online orders, bookings, or consultations that result from the site.
- Lead quality: Qualified inquiries that move smoothly through your sales funnel.
- Efficiency gains: Time saved in updating content, running campaigns, or supporting customers online.
- Long-term value: A site designed to adapt to new services, products, or regulatory changes without a full rebuild.
