### A Simple Comparison: Mobile-First vs Desktop-First
| Aspect | Mobile-First | Desktop-First |
|---|---|---|
| Primary screen size | Small screens first, then scale up | Desktop assumed, then adapt |
| Speed focus | High (LCP, TTI) | Moderate |
| Navigation | Thumb-friendly, bottom menus | Mouse-based, top menus |
| Conversion pathways | Simplified, single-tap actions | Multi-step forms often required |
| SEO impact | Strong local mobile signals | Desktop signals dominate historically |
