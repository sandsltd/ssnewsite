## The Three Core Pillars of Website Security

Think about security in terms of three big buckets: Protect, Detect, Respond. If you get these right, you’ll stop most breaches in their tracks and limit any impact you do experience. Saunders Simmons stitches these pillars into the very fabric of your site, not as a bolt-on afterthought.

### 1) Protect: Safe-by-Design Foundations

- Secure hosting and TLS/SSL as standard. A site should run over HTTPS by default, with modern ciphers and HSTS to prevent downgrade attacks. We’re not talking about a certificate once a year; we’re talking about ongoing certificate management and monitoring.
- Strong access control. Role-based access, two-factor authentication, and least-privilege permissions stop insider risk and messy breaches.
- Secure development practices. Even if you’re not building from scratch, we embed security checks into updates, plugins, and content management system (CMS) changes. It’s about designing out risk from day one.
- Regular vulnerability scanning. We scan routinely for vulnerabilities in themes, plugins, and configurations to catch issues before they become problems.

### 2) Detect: Know What’s Happening and When

- Centralised logging and monitoring. We collect and analyse logs so you spot anomalies rapidly. Early detection means smaller incidents with less downtime.
- Web Application Firewall (WAF) and threat intelligence. A WAF blocks common attack patterns and keeps you safer without slowing the user experience.
- Patch management. Keeping software, CMS, and plugins up to date is not glamourous, but it saves you from known exploits and avoids costly outages.

### 3) Respond: Be Ready When Things Go Wrong

- Automated backups and rapid recovery. Daily, tested backups mean you can restore quickly with minimal data loss. In Yeovil’s seasonal cycles—think Christmas peak and summer promotions—fast recovery matters.
- Incident response planning. A clear plan and a designated team mean you’re not firefighting in the moment; you’re executing a proven process.
- Post-incident review. We learn from every event, tightening controls and preventing repeated issues.

All of these elements work best when they’re aligned with your business goals and the realities of Yeovil’s market.
