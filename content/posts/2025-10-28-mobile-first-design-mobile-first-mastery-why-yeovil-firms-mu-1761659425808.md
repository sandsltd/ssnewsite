## Direct Answer: What Is Mobile-First Design and Why Should Yeovil Businesses Care?
Mobile-first design means starting the design and development process with the smallest screens in mind—phones—then scaling up to tablets and desktops. ## Quick Comparison: Mobile-First vs Desktop-First Design
| Aspect | Mobile-First Design | Desktop-First Design |
|---|---|---|
| Primary screen size | Small screens first | Large screens first |
| Page speed priority | High | Moderate |
| Navigation style | Simple, thumb-friendly | More complex, hover-based |
| Conversion focus | Clear CTAs on mobile | CTAs may lag on mobile |
| SEO emphasis | Mobile-friendly, local signals | Desktop signals dominate |
| Accessibility | Built-in priority for touch targets | Accessibility can be secondary |
