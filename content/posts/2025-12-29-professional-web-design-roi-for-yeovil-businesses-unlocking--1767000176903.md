## How We Measure ROI in Web Design

Now, you might be thinking, “What exactly should I measure to know if this is working?” Here’s a practical framework Saunders Simmons uses with Yeovil clients to measure ROI without turning your boardroom into a data lab.

### Direct conversion metrics
- Conversion rate (CR): The percentage of visitors who take a meaningful action (request a quote, complete a form, call your office).
- Cost per lead (CPL): How much you pay to acquire a single qualified lead via the website.
- Lead-to-sale ratio: The percentage of leads that convert into paying customers.

### Engagement and behavioural metrics
- Time on site and page depth: Indicates whether visitors are finding useful information and engaging with your content.
- Bounce rate on key pages: If users leave too quickly, your page needs clearer calls to action or better relevance.
- Scroll depth and click heatmaps: Show where people interact and where they lose interest—helping us refine layouts and messages.

### Performance and SEO metrics
- Page load speed: In Yeovil, even small delays can cost you conversions. We optimise Core Web Vitals to keep users engaged.
- Organic search rankings for local queries: “web design Yeovil” or “Yeovil web design services” help attract nearby buyers.
- Local citations and map pack visibility: A strong local presence boosts footfall and inquiries from people nearby.

### Business outcomes that matter
- Revenue lift from improved e-commerce experiences or lead generation.
- Customer lifetime value and repeat business from strong onboarding and trust signals.
- Reduced marketing waste because the site aligns with campaigns and channels rather than fighting them.

The trick is to tie these metrics back to your goals. If your objective is to win more inbound inquiries from Yeovil clients in Somerset, we’ll map your site’s design decisions to that outcome and track it month by month.
