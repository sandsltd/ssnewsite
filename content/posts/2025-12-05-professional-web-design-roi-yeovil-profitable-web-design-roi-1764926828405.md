### How the ROI is measured (and why it matters)

In Yeovil, a well-designed site should be trackable from day one. We measure ROI using a simple, reliable framework:

- Conversion rate uplift: The percentage increase in desired actions (enquiries, calls, form submissions) after a redesign.
- Lead quality: The proportion of leads that match your ICP (ideal customer profile) and close as customers.
- Revenue per visitor: A view of how much each visitor contributes to sales over a given period.
- Cost per acquisition (CPA): Marketing spend divided by new customers, with the aim of driving CPA down as the site improves.
- Customer lifetime value (CLV): The long-term value of customers gained through a better online experience.
- SEO performance: Organic rankings, click-through rates, and traffic from core Yeovil and Somerset search terms.
