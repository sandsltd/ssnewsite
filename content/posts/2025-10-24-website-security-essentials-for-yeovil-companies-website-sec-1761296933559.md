### The Essential Security Stack Every Yeovil Business Needs

There’s no magic wand, but there is a proven stack of protections that keeps your site safe, compliant, and reliable. Below is the core setup we implement for Yeovil clients, designed to be robust, future-proof, and scalable as you grow.

- Asset Inventory & Patch Management
  - Know every plugin, theme, and service your site relies on and keep it up to date.
  - Regular patching reduces known vulnerabilities that hackers can exploit. This is especially important for e-commerce sites serving customers in Yeovil and the wider Somerset region.
- Perimeter Security: Firewall & Web Application Firewall (WAF)
  - A strong firewall stops unauthorised access before it reaches your platform. A WAF adds a layer of protection against common web attacks like SQL injection and cross-site scripting.
  - We tailor WAF rules to your stack, so legitimate Yeovil users aren’t blocked while attackers are kept out.
- Access Controls & MFA
  - Strict user roles, two-factor authentication, and principle of least privilege make it much harder for breaches to occur via stolen credentials.
  - This is a big win for teams in Yeovil who collaborate with external agencies or freelancers.
- Encryption at Rest & In Transit
  - SSL/TLS for data in transit and encryption for sensitive data stored on servers. It’s essential for protecting customer details on forms, payments, and accounts.
- Regular Backups & Disaster Recovery (DR)
  - Frequent, immutable backups stored securely offsite or in the cloud. We test restore procedures so you’re never guessing when something goes wrong.
  - Ransomware resilience is built in with clean, tested restores that keep downtime low and customer impact minimal.
- Vulnerability Scanning & Pen Testing (Pro-Active)
  - Routine scans identify weak points before bad actors find them. Periodic pen tests simulate real attacks to validate your defences.
- Incident Response & Tabletop Drills
  - A clear playbook means your team acts fast, containment happens quickly, and recovery is smoother. We tailor these drills to your Yeovil business and supply chain.
- Monitoring, Logging & Security Hygiene
  - Centralised monitoring alerts you to suspicious activity in real time. Regular log reviews help you understand what happened and what to fix.
- Secure Development Practices for New Builds
  - If you’re updating a site or building a new app, we bake security into the development lifecycle from day one so you’re not patching later.
