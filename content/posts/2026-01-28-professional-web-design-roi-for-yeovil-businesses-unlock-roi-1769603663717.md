## How We Measure And Prove ROI

Measuring ROI for a web design project is about clarity, not guesswork. We track a handful of key metrics and translate them into tangible business outcomes.

### Key Metrics To Track

- Conversions: product purchases, quote requests, sign-ups, or contact form submissions
- Lead quality: the percentage of inquiries that convert to paying customers
- Revenue per visitor: how much revenue each visitor contributes on average
- Average order value: uplift from improved product guidance and cross-sell prompts
- Speed and reliability metrics: page load time, time to first byte, uptime
- Engagement signals: time on site, page depth, bounce rate
- Accessibility and compliance metrics: WCAG conformance, accessibility error rates
- Organic visibility: keyword rankings in Yeovil and wider Somerset markets

These metrics aren’t just numbers; they’re the signals that demonstrate ROI. We translate them into a clear forecast and a realistic timeline for your business in Yeovil and Somerset.

ROI can be framed as: (Incremental Profit from New Website − Cost of Website) ÷ Cost of Website × 100.
