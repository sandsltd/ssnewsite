## The Essentials: What A Robust Security Stack Looks Like

Below is the baseline we implement for Yeovil businesses. It’s practical, cost-effective, and designed to work with your existing tech stack—without turning your day into a security boot camp. And yes, you’ll see how these pieces slot into the broader web design and development process we offer.

### Secure Hosting And HTTPS Everywhere
- A secure hosting environment is more than just the server. It’s about isolation, backups, monitored uptime, and rapid recovery. We select hosting environments with resilient architectures and robust physical and cyber protections.
- HTTPS everywhere is non-negotiable. That means TLS certificates that are valid, up-to-date, and configured correctly to avoid mixed content issues that slow pages or leak data. In Yeovil, customers expect to see the padlock icon and know their data is protected when filling out forms or checking out.
- Work with a partner who manages certificate lifecycles, renewal notices, and protocol best practices so you never have to worry about expired certificates or deprecated ciphers slowing you down.

### Patch Management And Platform Updates
- Your CMS, plugins, and server software must be updated regularly. We establish a safe, tested patching cadence to close vulnerabilities without breaking your site.
- We prioritise updates that fix known exploits and improve security features, and we test them in a staging environment before going live.
- This isn’t a one-off task; it’s an ongoing discipline that protects you against now-known threats and reduces incident risk across the year.

### Strong Access Control And Authentication
- Two-factor authentication (2FA) for admin accounts is a must. It dramatically reduces the chance of credential-based breaches and is now a standard expectation for UK businesses.
- Role-based access means team members only see what they need to do their jobs. Least privilege reduces risk by limiting exposure.
- Password hygiene is still a thing: we enforce long, unique passwords and regular rotation. We don’t design around weak credentials; we design with them out of the equation.

We can integrate secure access controls seamlessly with your existing team and processes, keeping things simple for staff while raising protection levels.

### Web Application Firewall (WAF) And DDoS Protection
- A WAF sits in front of your site to block common attack patterns, SQL injections, and cross-site scripting attempts. It adds a protective layer without slowing legitimate traffic when tuned correctly.
- DDoS protection helps keep your site available during malicious traffic spikes—important for local businesses with seasonal peaks in Yeovil and the surrounding Somerset area.
- We configure rules that suit your site’s specific tech stack, so protection is tight but not a barrier to legitimate customers.
