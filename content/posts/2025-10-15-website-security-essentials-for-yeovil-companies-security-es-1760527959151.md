### Strong Access Controls and MFA

Only the right people should access your website admin panel. We implement robust access controls and multi‑factor authentication (MFA) for all administrative accounts. MFA might feel a bit inconvenient at times, but it dramatically reduces the risk of credential stuffing and account takeovers—issues that have plagued many UK businesses in recent years. For Yeovil companies, MFA is a quick win that protects customer data and internal operations alike. In practice, we configure role‑based access, enforce strong password policies, and set up MFA across admin interfaces and critical services.

We’ll often pair MFA with secure single sign‑on (SSO) options for larger teams, ensuring a seamless, secure experience for staff in Yeovil offices or remote working setups. This is the kind of security discipline that a professional partner like Saunders Simmons brings—consistent, auditable, and scalable as your team grows.

## Frequently Asked Questions (People Also Ask)

### What is website security and why should Yeovil businesses care?
Website security is the set of practices that protect a site from unauthorised access, data loss, and reputational harm. For Yeovil businesses, robust security protects customer data, supports legal compliance (GDPR), and keeps online operations smooth during peak shopping times in Somerset.

### Do I really need MFA for my website admin? Isn’t a strong password enough?
Passwords alone aren’t enough. MFA dramatically reduces the risk of credential theft and account compromise, which is especially important for small businesses that can’t afford downtime during busy periods.

### How often should backups be tested?
Backups should be tested at least quarterly, with a full restoration test annually. In practice, a professional partner runs regular restore drills so you’re never caught out by a failed recovery.

### Is SSL still essential for websites in 2025?
Absolutely. SSL/TLS encrypts data in transit, helps with Google rankings, and signals trust to visitors. It’s a non‑negotiable for any business handling customer information or payments.
